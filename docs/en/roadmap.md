# Roadmap

The Chinese document is the normative source; this page is its official translation.

## v0.1: baseline product

Core/schema, Existing Asset, Image/Video Draft, Doctor, MuJoCo/Three.js Compiler, local CAS, and L0–L3 reports. This repository currently completes the documentation/schema baseline and the first Rust Validator.

## Future candidates

1. **Profile expansion**: Isaac Sim/PhysX, Genesis, SDF/Gazebo, and Drake.
2. **Registry**: OCI distribution, signing, dependency resolution, enterprise access, and semantic Diff/Merge.
3. **Marketplace**: licensing, billing, versions, quality ratings, and supplier governance.
4. **World Generation**: procedural layouts, asset composition, constraint solving, and diversity audits.
5. **Task/Benchmark**: TaskSpec, Episode, Predicate Monitor, solvability certificates, and cross-backend evaluation.
6. **Complex physics**: deformables, cloth, fluid, particles, and dynamic-topology events.
7. **Real calibration**: active acquisition, system identification, and Sim2Real behavior calibration.

Progression requires measured v0.1 evidence that target loading, human cost, behavior deviation, and rights workflows are acceptable; it is not automatic by date.
