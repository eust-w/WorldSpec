# ADR-0008: Licensing and contributions

The Chinese document is the normative source; this page is its official translation.

## Decision

The Open Core uses dual AGPL-3.0 and commercial licensing. External contributions use a CLA granting the project rights needed for relicensing.

## Consequences

Legal counsel must produce final legal text; repository notes are not a complete license. Third-party code, models, weights, data, and assets receive separate RightsSpec/notices, and unknown rights block commercial release.
