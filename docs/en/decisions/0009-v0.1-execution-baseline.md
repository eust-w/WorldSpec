# ADR-0009: v0.1 execution and real-kitchen acceptance baseline

The Chinese document is the normative source; this page is its official translation.

## Decision

The v0.1 local platform uses a Rust Axum control plane and independent Python OCI workers. The control plane listens on loopback by default and uses a file CAS with SQLite WAL. Workers exchange data only through versioned Job/Result manifests and content digests; they do not share implicit paths.

The first L3 case uses a floating-base H1 with a Robotiq 2F-85. After walking 1–2 metres, H1 must open a drawer, grasp a mug, place and release the mug in the drawer, and close the drawer. The fixed initial state must pass five consecutive runs.

Physical-task evidence, 3DGS visual-shell evidence, and physical-invariance evidence with the shell disabled and enabled are produced separately. The real-kitchen case is complete only when all three pass. Private, non-commercial, or camera-coverage-failing kitchens may only serve as restricted evidence or negative examples.

## Consequences

- Without real GPU execution, a rights-cleared kitchen source, camera coverage, or a successful witness, the corresponding stage remains unaccepted.
- Mocks are limited to ordinary CI and cannot replace GPU, browser, native MuJoCo-load, or L3 behavior evidence.
- Feature-branch development and internal validation may proceed before legal review, but public release or merge to a release branch is prohibited.
