# Licensing and contribution governance

The Chinese document is the normative source; this page is its official translation.

## Accepted strategy

- Open Core: AGPL-3.0.
- Commercial use: separate commercial license.
- External contributions: a CLA granting the project the rights required to relicense.

This is a product and governance decision only. Legal counsel must review the final AGPL notices, commercial license, CLA, privacy terms, and liability boundaries.

## Third-party inheritance

Every SourceBundle, model weight, generation backend, dataset, texture, and asset records license, source URL/digest, author/vendor, usage restrictions, and redistribution status. A code license does not automatically grant resale rights for input or output assets.

Generation adapters lock code, weights, and data terms together. “Open-source repository” does not imply every weight or output is commercially usable.

## Release gate

A real kitchen with unknown rights may have only a manifest/evidence placeholder; assets are not copied, uploaded, or published. License checks do not provide final legal conclusions. `unknown/review_required` blocks commercial distribution.
