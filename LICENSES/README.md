# License files pending legal review

The accepted licensing strategy is AGPL-3.0 for the open core, a separate commercial license, and a CLA that permits relicensing of external contributions.

No final license or CLA text is included in this baseline. Before public distribution, legal counsel must approve and add:

- the exact AGPL-3.0 license notice and full text;
- the commercial license agreement;
- the contributor license agreement;
- third-party notices for models, code, datasets, and generated assets.

See `docs/zh-CN/governance/licensing.md` and its English mirror.
